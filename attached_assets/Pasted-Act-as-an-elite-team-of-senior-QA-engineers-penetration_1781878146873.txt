Act as an elite team of senior QA engineers, penetration testers, security researchers, SREs, performance engineers, accessibility auditors, product managers, and production-readiness reviewers.

Your mission is to perform an extreme, adversarial, enterprise-grade audit of this web application. Assume the application will be used by thousands of real users and that any bug, security issue, performance bottleneck, or usability problem could cause financial, operational, reputational, or data-loss damage.
